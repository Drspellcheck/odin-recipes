Chicken Cutlet in Sun-Dried Tomato Cream Sauce

Description

This dish is a summer time classic! The robust flavor of the
Sun-Dried Tomato Cream sauce is sure delight anyone who tatses
it. This dish is also nearly a one pot dish, so its sure to be
a favorite go to in a pinch!

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken cutlets
  • 1/4 tsp salt, divided
  • 1/4 tsp ground pepper, divided
  • 1/2 cup slivered oil-packed sun-dried tomatos, plus 1 tbsp
    oil from the jar
  • 1/2 cup finaly shopped shallots
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Sprinkle chicken witb 1/8 tsp each salt and pepper. Heat
    sun-dried tomato oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the
    chicken and cook, turning once, until browned and a instant-read
    thermometer inserted int o the thicket part regfisters 165℉,
    about 6 minutes total. Transfer to plate.

  2. Add sun-dried tomatos and shallots to the pan. Cook,stirring,
    for 1 minute. Increase heat to high and add wine. Cook, scraping up any
    browned bits,until the liquid has mostly evaporated, about 2 minutes.
    Reduce heat to medium and stir in cream, any accumulated juices from
    the chicken and the remaining 1/8 teaspoon each salt and pepper; simmer
    for 2 minutes. Return chicken to the pan and turnj to coat with the sauce.
    Serve the chicken topped with the sauce and parsley.
