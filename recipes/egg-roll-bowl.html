Egg Roll In a Bowl

Description

Egg roll in a bowl is a perfect quick and easy one pot meal. Take some ground pork,
grab a bag of cole slaw mix and viola! This dish can easily be prepared in less than
30 minuutes and the whole family will be raving!

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp toastred sesame oil
  • 3 cloves garlic,minced
  • 1/2 cup onion,diced
  • 5 green onions,sliced on bias(white and green parts)
  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • sea salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tbsp Sriracha or green chili sauce, more to taste
  • 14 oz bag coleslaw mix
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp unseasoned rice vinegar
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • Yum Yum Sauce

Steps

  1. Heat the sesame oil in large skillet over medium high heat.
  2. Add the garlic, onion, and white portion of the green onions
    Saute until the onions are translucent and the garlic is fragrant.
  3. Add the ground pork, ginger, salt, pepper, and Sriracha. Saute
    until the pork is cooked through.
  4. Add the coleslaw mix, soy suace, and rice vinegar. Saute until
    the coleslaw is tender.
  5. Top with green onions and sesame seeds before serving.
